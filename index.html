ABOUT SUNRIDGE COUNTY

Sunridge County is an original low-poly open-world action adventure built as a single HTML file. Every building, vehicle, character, texture, sound and note of music is generated by code at load time — there are no external art, model or audio files.

The look is a deliberate throwback: flat-shaded polygons, 64-pixel textures, hard fog, blob shadows and a chunky bevelled HUD. The design is a throwback too — a hand-made county, a story told in four acts, and side work you find by driving around.
